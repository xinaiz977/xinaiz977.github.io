My Research

My research interests are mainly focused on biomechanical analysis, mechanical design, and material characterization. Each project has led me toward the same fundamental question: how do we make engineered systems behave reliably within the complexity of biological reality? From tuning material properties at the microscale to programming mechanical stimulation for engineered tissues, my work has consistently focused on designing and validating clinically relevant systems that interact precisely with biological environments.

Research Skills: MATLAB, LabVIEW, SolidWorks, Autodesk Fusion 360, CAD, Python, C++, Arduino, 3D Printing, Mechanical Testing, Material Processing, Statistical Analysis, Cell-Based Assays, Cell Passaging, Genotyping, Western Blotting
